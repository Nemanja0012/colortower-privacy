Privacy Policy for Color Tower

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Color Tower ("the game", "we", "us") is a block stacking game for Android. The package name is com.colortower.game. This policy explains what information the game uses and what happens to it. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

Short version: the game has no accounts, we do not collect your name, email or location ourselves, and your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data for that, as described below.

1. Who we are

The game is made by Nemanja Tanaskovic. You can contact us at mezzaninegames@mezgam.onworkspace.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress on your device only. This includes your best score, your best height, your sound and reduce motion settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial. This data is never sent to us or to any server. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, it is deleted.

3. Information we collect ourselves

None. The game has no sign-up, no login, no chat and no online features of our own. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers that receive data from the game.

4. Ads and Google AdMob

The game is free and is supported by ads. We use Google AdMob, a service of Google, to show three kinds of ads:

  • banner ads at the bottom of the screen,
  • full screen ads that can appear between games,
  • optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to get a bonus, such as a revive.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data from your device. Depending on your settings and where you live, this can include:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings),
  • your IP address and the approximate location that comes from it,
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version,
  • information about how you interact with ads, such as views and taps,
  • crash and diagnostic information related to ads.

We do not receive this data and we do not combine it with anything else. Google processes it under its own policies. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Personalized ads

Ads may be personalized, which means they are chosen using your advertising ID and interests, or non-personalized, which means they are chosen with less data. You can control this on your phone:

  • Android settings, then Google, then Ads, where you can opt out of ad personalization or reset your advertising ID.
  • In the game, if you are in a region where consent is required, you can change your choice with the Privacy settings button.

5. Consent in Europe and the UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown. The consent message comes from Google's consent tool. You can say yes or no. You can change your mind at any time with the Privacy settings button in the game. If you do not consent, the game still works, but you may see less relevant ads.

6. Children

Color Tower is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and think a child has given personal information to a service connected to the game, please contact us at mezzaninegames@mezgam.onworkspace.com and we will help.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google, you can use the controls in section 4 and Google's own privacy tools at https://myaccount.google.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you may also have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its use and to complain to your local data protection authority. For data about ads, those rights are exercised with Google.

8. Security

Saved progress stays inside the game's private storage on your phone. Ads are loaded over secure connections by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

9. Third party services

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). It does not use any other analytics, social media or tracking service.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because we add a new feature or service, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is important, we will also tell you in the game or in the store listing.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: mezzaninegames@mezgam.onworkspace.com
